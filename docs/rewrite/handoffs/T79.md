# T79 — Modular office floor tutorial revision

Status: **Complete**, 0.57.0 · C52.

[Tutorial 7](../../tutorials/videos/07-office-floor.mp4) replaces the earlier exercise with
42 × 18 m of office floor: a 6 × 6 × 3.6 m frame multiplied through its volumetric
instance hotspot into 21 modules, two U stairs, four lift cases, service rooms,
12 door openings, north/south glazed arrays and a partial repeated ceiling.
The [owner's drawings](../../tutorials/tutorial-07-plan.md),
[interpretation](../../tutorials/tutorial-07-revision.md),
[recipe](../../tutorials/recipes/07-office-floor.py),
[complete model](../../tutorials/projects/07-office-floor.octd) and
[structural checkpoint](../../tutorials/projects/07-office-floor-structure.octd) are versioned.
The detailed drawing's kitchen/storage/copier occupies F–G; the meeting room occupies
G–H to preserve both programs. FR30/FR90 annotations represent the requested design intent.

The tutorial exposed a numeric inspector bug: a row-centre click hit a stepper, and
an old field's blur could clear the newly focused editor. Measured `setting-input/`
bounds now identify editable widgets, paired fields share available width, and blur
only clears its own active key. The native regression failed before the fix (`0.5`
became `0.35`) and passes eight alternating edits afterwards.
[Before/after evidence](../references/tutorial-settings/README.md).
The recipe locks annotations while making the curtain-wall object, preserving all
31 root labels. Curtain-wall panels have two vertical posts and glazing, with no top rail.
