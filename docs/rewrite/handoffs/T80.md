# T80 — Correct office stair circulation

Status: **Complete**, 0.57.1 · C53.

Owner review found that the return flights did not meet their mid-landings and
there was no usable floor-level approach. A native reopen of the released 0.57.0
model measured only **15 cm** to the generated wall face. Both flights ascended
in the same plan direction, and the upper flight mesh missed the landing.
[Before evidence](../references/office-stairs/README.md).

The corrected [recipe](../../tutorials/recipes/07-office-floor.py) draws
opposite-rising 3.15 m flights with twelve 150 mm risers each. Full-width
mid-landings join the flights at 1.8 m. Each entrance has **1.35 m of physical
clearance**, and the stair doors open into that clear floor area. The east stair
copies by 33 units so its wider landing stays inside the enclosure; its label
moves into the new gap between flights. Core boundary walls align with the
middle grid row, and the meeting entrance follows the adjusted north wall.

The underlying direction bug was in line normalization: canonical-axis clustering
emitted every segment forward, discarding how it was drawn. Emitted segments now
follow the retained source orientation, including overlap consolidation and split
fragments. Regression `drawn_line_preserves_direction_through_overlap_and_splitting`
failed before the fix (Z=12 → 0 became Z=0 → 12), then passed after it. Existing
geometry is not silently rewritten; the tutorial model is rebuilt from its recipe.
