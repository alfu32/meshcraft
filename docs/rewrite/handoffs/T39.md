# T39 handoff

Status: **Planned for the replacement stack**. Earlier egui/Bevy renderer results are historical only; port the accepted rendering requirements to raylib-rs and verify them under C39. T17 provides command-backed settings; C24 retains capture coverage.

## Historical implementation evidence (retired egui/Bevy path)

The old painter prototype implemented darker/bluer back faces, alpha preservation, transformed scene occurrences, a bounded CPU depth map, 3×3 PCF-style depth comparisons and ground/entity receivers. Tests covered orientation propagation across triangulated faces, coincident-position vertices, self-shadow bias and occlusion. These results do not meet the selected production GPU-renderer architecture and are not accepted as C39 completion.

## Current requirements

raylib-rs must use shadow maps for solid geometry casting onto ground and other entities. Keep authored lines, grid, selection and preview overlays outside the caster pass; preserve front/back tint and transparency. Carry lighting/shadow settings and inclusion-aware PNG/SVG capture through the new backend. Verify transformed occurrences, scale, bias, transparency and fixed-camera results under C24/C39. Do not implement a per-frame CPU depth or framebuffer copy as the production viewport path.
