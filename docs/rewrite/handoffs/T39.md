# T39 handoff

Status: **In progress**. The production raylib offscreen viewport now has GPU directional lighting and cached shadow mapping. Broader lighting persistence/conversion and view capture remain open under C24/T17; earlier egui/Bevy results remain historical.

## Historical implementation evidence (retired egui/Bevy path)

The old painter prototype implemented darker/bluer back faces, alpha preservation, transformed scene occurrences, a bounded CPU depth map, 3×3 PCF-style depth comparisons and ground/entity receivers. Tests covered orientation propagation across triangulated faces, coincident-position vertices, self-shadow bias and occlusion. These results do not meet the selected production GPU-renderer architecture and are not accepted as C39 completion.

## Current requirements

raylib-rs must use shadow maps for solid geometry casting onto ground and other entities. Keep authored lines, grid, selection and preview overlays outside the caster pass; preserve front/back tint and transparency. Carry lighting/shadow settings and inclusion-aware PNG/SVG capture through the new backend. Verify transformed occurrences, scale, bias, transparency and fixed-camera results under C24/C39. Do not implement a per-frame CPU depth or framebuffer copy as the production viewport path.

## Current raylib implementation — 2026-10-19 (#13, #21)

- `apps/desktop/src/gpui_lighting.rs` caches resolved, transformed geometry by immutable document snapshot. It batches solid triangles with one shader, then draws authored black lines and selection highlights outside that shader. Scene helpers, cursor and previews remain outside the caster pass.
- An orthographic light camera renders opaque solid faces into a 2048² RGBA target with hardware depth testing and packed 24-bit depth. The main GLSL pass compares the light-space depth with 3 × 3 PCF. The light map refreshes on document/light-direction changes, not pointer movement, selection tint or viewer orbit. There is no CPU shadow map.
- Flat face normals come from world-position derivatives; ambient/directional/specular contributions vary with the light direction. Back faces retain a darker, bluer tint. A small eye-depth-aware filled-surface bias keeps coplanar line overlays readable. Ground and other solid entities receive shadows; lines/guides never cast shadows. Transparent faces preserve their authored alpha and are excluded from the opaque caster pass.
- The existing Shader Settings section now adjusts ambient, directional and specular levels, shadow strength, normalized depth/normal bias, light azimuth/elevation, and a shadow toggle. Defaults use k3d's ambient 0.59, directional 0.73, specular 0.20 and shadow alpha 0.50. These controls are currently session-local; raw legacy 2500/5620 bias values are not assigned to normalized GPU bias. CSM/dither are not advertised as implemented controls.

### Verification

`env -u WAYLAND_DISPLAY cargo test -p meshcraft-desktop --lib -- --include-ignored --test-threads=1` passes **231 tests**, including the explicitly display-dependent GPU regression. Its fixed-camera comparison covers both receivers separately (1,511 ground pixels and 1,774 entity pixels darkened in the probe scene), shader compile/link success, and black line composition. [GPU receiver evidence](../references/meshcraft-raylib-shadow-receivers.png) shows the floating caster's ground shadow and a second caster shading the platform.

`env -u WAYLAND_DISPLAY cargo run -p meshcraft-desktop --bin meshcraft -- --windowed --size 1800x950` builds and launches the complete GPUI shell. [Live scene evidence](../references/meshcraft-gpui-generated-icons-lit-scene.png) shows generated command icons, a shaded cone, ground shadow, selection and construction feedback. This is a live capture, not a controlled dense-scene timing benchmark. `cargo fmt --all -- --check` and the plan verifier pass.

Remaining T39 acceptance: persisted legacy lighting conversion, capture/PNG/SVG completion, transparent sorting and large-scene/shadow quality bounds; full fixed-camera C24 coverage remains open.
