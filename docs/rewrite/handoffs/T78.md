## Delivered

- A 27 × 15 m office floor, drafted from Architecture at 30 cm per unit. One central
  circulation lobby connects four office zones. Four copied partitions form repeatable
  bays, three matching doors occupy real openings, and forty 600 mm ceiling panels
  share one definition at 2.7 m. The ceiling stays partial to show the floor layout.
- Every modeling pick uses a verified native snap. Upward movement follows an axis
  helper, the panel is window-selected before Make Object, and the long panel repeat
  shows typed input as the secondary method. A traced dry run verified 51 snap aims.
- The final orbit checks all eight corners of the model bounds against a 20 px
  viewport margin. Frame review caught a clipped slab corner before this check existed.
- Ground rendering fix: the synthetic ground uses a small constant clip-depth bias
  behind authored faces. Its previous eye-depth-dependent bias became smaller than
  a depth-buffer sample at architectural camera distances, causing blue ground to
  show through the floor. Model positions and instanced mesh fill bias are unchanged.

## Recording and review

`python3 scripts/video/build.py --only 07` passed its live model checks, then
`python3 scripts/video/assemble.py --only 07` assembled the sound through FilmCraft.
The finished video is 321.733 seconds, 1920 × 1080, with 19 narration cues and exactly
one H.264 video, AAC audio and selectable English `mov_text` subtitle track. SRT/VTT
sidecars are supplied; mpv activates one subtitle set. Subtitles are not burned in.

The media checks used the following commands (and the same commands for tutorial 6):

```sh
ffprobe -v error -show_streams -show_format -of json docs/tutorials/videos/07-office-floor.mp4
ffmpeg -hide_banner -i docs/tutorials/videos/07-office-floor.mp4 -map 0:a -af ebur128 -f null -
mpv --no-config --no-audio --vo=null --frames=1 --sub-auto=exact docs/tutorials/videos/07-office-floor.mp4
```

## Reboot recovery and limits

An unexpected GPU-related reboot stopped speech generation after 14 clips. All 14
cached FLACs passed full decoding with `ffmpeg -v error -i CLIP -f null -`.
`TTS_DEVICE=CPU QWEN_CODEC_CPU=1 python3 scripts/video/voice.py` completed the five
missing clips with the same local Ryan model. This recovery command is documented
in the authoring guide. No recording overlapped speech generation or compilation.

Audio has **not been listened to** by the author of this work. Listen especially to
lines 1 (units), 8 (door dimensions), 12 (partition spacing; its first synthesis was
rejected and retried), and 17 (the repeat count). Sampled frames were reviewed, not
every frame. Native visual validation was on Linux with software rendering.

The node is a horizontal circulation lobby; this schematic exercise does not add
stairs, lifts or services. Ceiling panels are flat drafting objects. No building-code
approval is claimed. GitHub issues were checked: #27 remains an open rendering
feature; no open bug issue was present. This ground-depth fix does not resolve #27.
