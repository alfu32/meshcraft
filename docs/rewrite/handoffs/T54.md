# T54 handoff — Persistent oriented cursor and context-safe guide placement (#8, #9, #14, #15)

Status: **In progress**. The legacy egui behavior is now carried into the Bevy viewport; visual comparison and final acceptance remain open.

## Implemented

- The native viewport shares its geometry/grid snap result with the session. A segment snap uses the revision-keyed spatial index to recover the segment direction; the cursor basis keeps blue along that segment, red parallel to the ground, and green orthogonal to both.
- Bevy gizmos draw short colored axis segments without arrowheads. A small screen-space center dot is synchronized after the 3D overlays, and directly selected vertices receive small red screen-space bullets.
- G and T place grid and axis guides at the cached cursor through the shared command path. The shortcuts are ignored over interactive UI, while a palette/context menu is open, during toolbar dragging, or with modifiers; they preserve the active tool and staged input.
- Guide visibility and Escape clearing use the shared session state. Cursor and guide overlays do not enter the shadow pass; authored line batches also remain non-shadow-casters.

## Remaining acceptance

- Visually compare segment orientation, cursor center, selected-vertex bullets and both guide types against the reference application at different camera angles and zoom levels.
- Recheck guide placement while staged tools retain points, when the cursor has no valid snap, and after Escape clears selection or guides.
- Close issues #14 and #15 only after visual review confirms the screen-space markers stay legible and line overlays do not interfere with selection or shadow rendering.

## Validation

Focused tests pass for the spatially indexed segment cursor axis, both native G/T guide shortcuts, UI-interaction shortcut suppression, selected-vertex world-position resolution, and cursor-dot screen-coordinate placement/hiding.
