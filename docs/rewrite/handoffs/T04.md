# T04 handoff

Status: in progress; T03 is complete.

- Added `transactions::PendingTransaction` with begin, validated staging/preview, commit, and cancel boundaries. A failed stage leaves its draft unchanged; a failed or stale commit leaves the live document and history unchanged.
- `TransactionHistory` stores bounded before/after document states, one `ChangeSet` and label per explicit commit, redo branching, a 20-operation cap, and a 64 MiB serialized-state budget. Oldest states are pruned; an oversized before/after pair rejects the operation. Undo and redo keep the document revision monotonic. A `HistoryClock` makes timestamps deterministic in tests.
- Desktop geometry, object, rename, model-setting, and new-document actions now share this history. Undo/redo are exposed in the Edit menu and command palette. Selection is pruned after restore. Camera, lighting/shadow controls, and application preferences remain outside authored document history; model settings are included.
- `OpaqueLegacyHistory` retains the envelope index, labels, timestamps, encoded strings, and unknown fields. T05's `LegacySnapshotAdapter` now supplies lazy standard Base64/gzip decoding; corrupt entries keep their bytes and receive diagnostics without mutating a live document.
