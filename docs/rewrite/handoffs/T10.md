# T10 handoff

Status: in progress; T07 remains in progress. This records the perspective and orthographic camera slices without claiming full T10 completion.

`Viewport` now has a perspective orbit camera with target, orbit distance, camera-space pan, cursor-anchored wheel zoom, and target-centered fit-to-document. Ground-plane mapping unprojects a camera ray. Near-camera geometry is excluded from drawing and picking. Six orthographic bases retain constant screen scale along camera depth; the same projection drives rendering, depth ordering, fit-to-document, and pixel-space picking. Six `view.ortho.*` commands switch views and expose checked state without mutating the document. The legacy orthographic command selects top view; Orbit returns to perspective and reset restores the default perspective pose.

Ground-plane tools receive valid coordinates in perspective and top/bottom views. Side/front/back views report that the ground-plane operation is unsupported instead of fabricating a point. Height entry is rejected when the active projection cannot show vertical height.

Checks: `cargo test -p meshcraft-desktop --lib` passes (46 tests) and covers all six projection bases, constant orthographic scale, perspective depth scaling and ground-plane inversion, target-centered framing at large world offsets, orthographic picking, visible-height inversion, and command dispatch/state.

Changed paths: `apps/desktop/src/{app.rs,viewport.rs}`, `docs/rewrite/handoffs/{T06.md,T10.md}`, `docs/rewrite/plan.md`.

Remaining: walkthrough movement/support/gravity/jump preferences, camera persistence boundaries, six-view construction planes, focus/DPI/menu mapping, and full C09 checks. Keep T10 in progress.
