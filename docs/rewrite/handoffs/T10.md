WASD moves on the horizontal plane, Left/Right strafe, Up/Down adjust eye offset, Space jumps, right-drag looks, middle/Shift-right drag pans, and wheel moves along the view direction. Perspective construction uses the Y=0 plane; walkthrough uses its active camera ray. Height entry is rejected when the active projection cannot show vertical height.
