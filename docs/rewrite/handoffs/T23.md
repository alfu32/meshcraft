## 0.15.2 — Constant-section miter joins

A stepped profile exposed tapering at path fractures: the previous generator
rigidly rotated a unit-size section onto the bisector, shrinking its projected
width by `cos(turn / 2)`. Planar/spatial sweeps and Ribbon 3D now use an oblique
miter section, stretching only the bend-plane component by the reciprocal.
The join is shared/welded by both spans, including a closed path's first join;
profile coordinates are measured against the first span. Spatial orientation is
transported along actual span tangents, making collinear subdivision stable.
Closed-loop roll correction is weighted by distance rather than point count.

Research: [Open CASCADE RightCorner transition](https://occt3d.com/dev/doc/refman/html/class_b_rep_offset_a_p_i___make_pipe_shell.html)
extends/intersects neighboring sweeps at spine fractures. The native triangle
implementation follows that geometric principle without adding a CAD kernel.
Round/bevel styles and global self-intersection/Boolean cleanup remain outside
this correction. Reversals/miters over a ratio of 64 and locally reversed offset
rails are rejected explicitly; output is never returned partially. Existing
surface/cap semantics are retained. Authored meshes from older sweeps must be
regenerated to gain the new joins.
